# Keyboard-Only Dungeon Crawler

Dungeon is a small keyboard-only dungeon crawler where typing is your main way of moving.

Find your way through the maze, choose your path by typing directional words, and escape before the enemy catches you.

## How to Play

1. Press **Enter** to start the game.
2. Four words will appear around the typing area, one for each direction:
   - Up
   - Down
   - Left
   - Right
3. Type the word for the direction you want to move.
4. Press **Enter** or **Space** to confirm the move.
5. The player moves through the corridor until reaching a wall, dead end, junction, or exit.
6. Keep moving and stay ahead of the enemy.
7. Reach the exit before you are caught.

## Controls

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Letters | Type the direction word |
| Enter | Confirm the typed word and move |
| Space | Confirm the typed word and move |
| Backspace | Delete the last letter |
| R | Restart after winning or getting caught |
| Tab | Does nothing |

## Features

- Keyboard-only gameplay
- No mouse interaction
- Branching maze with different paths
- Corridor-based movement
- Smooth player movement
- Typing-based movement
- Four directional word choices
- Compass-style direction layout
- Random words
- Words change after successful moves
- Wall collision
- Exit tile and level completion
- Enemy chase system
- Enemy follows the player through the maze
- Game-over and restart system
